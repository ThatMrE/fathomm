The concept

What is medical tourism?

Crossing a border to get care that's cheaper, faster, or simply unavailable at home — a mature, multi-billion-dollar global market that runs on one scarce resource: trust.

People travel for four reasons, usually together. The hard part isn't the flight — it's knowing whether the clinic is any good, what it truly costs, and what happens if something goes wrong. That gap is the whole problem Fathomm exists to solve.

💰

Cost

Procedures run 40–90% cheaper abroad. A $52,000 U.S. full-mouth restoration is ~$18,000 in Baja.

🔑

Access

Treatments that are restricted, backlogged, or unavailable at home — from electives to the experimental frontier.

🏅

Expertise

🌿

Experience

Recovery somewhere restorative — a calm, low-exposure setting and coordinated aftercare built around the procedure.

Methodology

The Fathomm Trust Score

One number, 0–100, weighted so a beautifully marketed clinic offering an unproven treatment can never outrank a modest clinic doing a well-established one.

Because evidence carries the most weight, the score protects patients where the market fails them most: the gap between how a clinic looks and whether its treatment actually works.

Onboarding

How Fathomm works

Six steps. Each one removes a specific source of risk — turning an information-and-logistics problem into a considered journey.

Case study
"A short trip
for a new smile."

Marcus, 54, San Diego. Needs a full-arch dental restoration. U.S. quote: $52,000. Insurance covers almost none of it — and he doesn't know how to tell a good Tijuana clinic from a bad one.

01 · Discover & match

From a goal, not a clinic

Marcus filters Fathomm for dentistry and Trust Tier A–B. Instead of the cheapest option in Los Algodones, Fathomm surfaces two vetted Baja clinics — a Rosarito practice open since 1992 and a Zona Río Tijuana clinic whose lead dentist holds a California license. Both are a short drive across the border. Estimated all-in: ~$18,000 for both arches.

02 · Vet

Credentials, verified in writing

Fathomm confirms the treating dentist's California license and AACD membership, pulls verified reviews, and secures a single itemized quote — implants, prostheses, imaging, sedation, and a five-year warranty. No "implants-only" bait pricing, no hidden broker markup.

03 · The trip

San Diego → the Baja clinic

Fathomm arranges door-to-door ground transport, lodging near the clinic, and border logistics. Treatment is scheduled across two short in-clinic days, surgical phase first, with a Spanish-speaking liaison handling transfers.

04 · Recover & follow-up

Rest, then a clear path home

After placement, Marcus recovers in a vetted nearby hotel — a soft-food menu, low infection exposure, a nurse check-in — then returns to San Diego. The crown phase is booked as a second, shorter trip months later, with a local dentist on call for anything in between.

The business

Scaling the trusted navigator

Fathomm's moat is credibility. It starts where trust is verifiable, then expands across what's genuinely reputable.

01

First market: Baja

The Baja dental corridor is ideal: verifiable quality, huge savings, a short drive from San Diego, and a vast uninsured U.S. audience. Prove the model, then template it.

02

Clusters of credibility

Add A/B-tier clusters in sequence — Cancún, Istanbul/Izmir, Busan/Jeju, the Riviera & Gulf longevity peers — expanding across what's genuinely reputable.

03

Monetize trust

Concierge & orchestration fees, vetted-clinic partnerships, premium vetting tiers — while the Trust Score stays free and independent, because credibility is the product.

04

The map is the front door

The atlas is top-of-funnel: patients arrive to explore prices, meet the Trust Score, and convert into onboarding — and see Fathomm rank down the dangerous fringe.

05

Guardrails as strategy

Fathomm declines to broker discredited care and labels the experimental. In a market that preys on the desperate, being the operator that says "no" is the durable advantage.

06

Recovery network

Every trip ends with a written aftercare plan and a home-country follow-up link — closing the gap that derails most solo medical trips and earning repeat trust.
