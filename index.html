Medical tourism around the world

A borderless market for care.

Crossing a border for treatment is no longer a fringe choice — it's a mature, fast-growing global market, expanding at double-digit rates and measured in millions of patients a year.

Rising home-country costs, long public waitlists, and world-class specialization abroad have turned a trickle into a tide. Care now clusters in a handful of regions, each with a signature strength.

Fast-rising premium destinations for advanced and specialist care, with heavy state investment in accredited, internationally-staffed hospitals.

Why the demand exists

Four forces push people abroad.

Patients rarely travel for one reason alone — cost, wait times, access, and expertise compound until leaving home becomes the rational choice.

💰

Cost

Procedures run 40–90% cheaper abroad. A hip replacement near $50,000 in the U.S. can be under $10,000 in India.

⏳

Wait times

Where public systems back up for months or years, an accredited clinic abroad can schedule care in weeks.

🔑

Access

Treatments that are restricted, backlogged, or simply unavailable at home — from routine electives to the frontier.

🏅

Expertise

Some destinations lead the world by volume: Türkiye for hair, Korea for contouring, India for cardiac surgery.
