Legal Framework

Legitimate by flag-state law

FATHOMM operates on a well-established principle of maritime law: in international waters, the law of the vessel's flag state governs — not the FDA.

International Waters

Beyond 12 nautical miles from San Francisco — roughly a 90-minute sail past the Golden Gate — U.S. federal health jurisdiction diminishes.

Flag-State Jurisdiction

Under UNCLOS, a vessel registered in a permissive jurisdiction is governed by that state's medical and pharmaceutical law while at sea.

Women on Waves Precedent

A Dutch-flagged medical vessel has operated lawfully in international waters for 20+ years — the strongest precedent for the model.

Compliance Architecture

Maritime legal counsel, a medical advisory board, rigorous clinical protocols, and robust informed-consent processes.
