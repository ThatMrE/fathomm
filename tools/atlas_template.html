The concept

What is medical tourism?

Crossing a border to get care that's cheaper, faster, or simply unavailable at home — a mature, multi-billion-dollar global market that runs on one scarce resource: trust.

People travel for four reasons, usually together. The hard part isn't the flight — it's knowing whether the clinic is any good, what it truly costs, and what happens if something goes wrong. That gap is the whole problem Fathomm exists to solve.

💰

Cost

Procedures run 40–90% cheaper abroad. A $52,000 U.S. full-mouth restoration is ~$18,000 in Baja.

🔑

Access

Treatments that your home system restricts, backlogs, or does not offer — from electives to the experimental frontier.

🏅

Expertise

Some places lead the world by sheer volume: Turkey for hair, Korea for facial contouring, Switzerland for longevity.

⚓

Experience

Recovery somewhere restorative. Fathomm's answer: recover at sea, on a voyage built around the procedure.

Methodology

The Fathomm Trust Score

One number, 0–100, weighted so a beautifully marketed clinic offering an unproven treatment can never outrank a modest clinic doing a well-established one.

Because evidence carries the most weight, the score protects patients where the market fails them most: the gap between how a clinic looks and whether its treatment actually works.

Onboarding

How a Fathomm voyage works

Six steps. Each one removes a specific source of risk — turning an information-and-logistics problem into a considered journey.

Case study
"A Pacific voyage
for a new smile."

Marcus, 54, San Diego. Needs a full-arch dental restoration. U.S. quote: $52,000. Insurance covers almost none of it — and he doesn't know how to tell a good Tijuana clinic from a bad one.

01 · Discover & match

From a goal, not a clinic

Marcus filters Fathomm for dentistry, coastal access, and Trust Tier A–B. Instead of the cheapest inland option in Los Algodones, Fathomm surfaces the Baja Pacific corridor — a Rosarito beachfront practice open since 1992 and a Zona Río Tijuana clinic whose lead dentist holds a California license. Both are a short sail from home. Estimated all-in: ~$18,000 for both arches.

02 · Vet

Credentials, verified in writing

Fathomm confirms the treating dentist's California license and AACD membership, pulls verified reviews, and secures a single itemized quote — implants, prostheses, imaging, sedation, and a five-year warranty. No "implants-only" bait pricing, no hidden broker markup.

03 · The voyage

San Diego → the Baja coast, by sea

Marcus boards a chartered yacht at Shelter Island. An easy day-sail down the coast reaches an Ensenada/Rosarito anchorage. The clinic schedules treatment across two short in-clinic days, surgical phase first, with a Spanish-speaking liaison handling transfers and the border.

04 · Recover at sea

A galley, not a hotel lobby

After placement, Marcus recovers aboard — calm water, a soft-food menu, low infection exposure, a nurse check-in. The yacht returns him to San Diego. The crown phase follows as a second, shorter voyage months later, with a local dentist on call for anything in between.

The business

Scaling the trusted navigator

Fathomm's moat is credibility. It starts where trust and coastline align, then expands along the shoreline of what's genuinely reputable.

01

Beachhead: Baja

The Pacific dental corridor is ideal: verifiable quality, huge savings, a short safe sail from San Diego, and a vast uninsured U.S. audience. Prove the voyage model, then template it.

02

Coast of credibility

Add A/B-tier coastal clusters in sequence — Cancún, Istanbul/Izmir, Busan/Jeju, the Riviera & Gulf longevity peers — with "fly-and-berth" hybrids where the best clinic is inland.

03

Monetize trust

Concierge & orchestration fees, vetted-clinic partnerships, voyage charters, premium vetting tiers — while the Trust Score stays free and independent, because credibility is the product.

04

The map is the front door

The atlas is top-of-funnel: patients arrive to explore prices, meet the Trust Score, and convert into onboarding — and see Fathomm rank down the dangerous fringe.

05

Guardrails as strategy

Fathomm declines to broker discredited care and labels the experimental. In a market that preys on the desperate, being the operator that says "no" is the durable advantage.

06

Recovery network

Every voyage ends with a written aftercare plan and a home-country follow-up link — closing the gap that derails most solo medical trips and earning repeat trust.
